# Elevation and focus

Three levels. Cards at rest use a border, not a shadow.

- **Elevation 1:** card hover, sticky bars.
- **Elevation 2:** menus, popovers, toasts.
- **Elevation 3:** modals and side panels.

**Focus ring:** a 2 px ring in `--air-border-focus` with a 2 px gap in the surface colour. It shows on keyboard focus only (`:focus-visible`), on every interactive component.
