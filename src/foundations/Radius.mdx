# Radius

One radius for almost everything: 4 px. Small, medium, large and extra large share that value, so the names say where a radius is used without changing the look. Extra small (2 px) is for parts inside parts; full is for round things like avatars and badges. The focus ring follows the control's corners, 4 px out.
